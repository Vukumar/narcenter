How We Work

  1. Enquiry & Quote Request

    Contact us by phone or WhatsApp and share photographs, material details, estimated quantities, and the site location.

  2. Site Inspection & Pricing

    We evaluate material type, condition, estimated quantity, and collection needs, then discuss the per-kilogram rate or agreed pricing structure.

  3. Pickup & Digital Weighing

    We coordinate loading, transport, and weighing at the agreed location or yard, explaining the weighing process openly.

  4. Immediate Payment

    After weighing and agreement on final quantity and price, payment is settled by the agreed cash, bank, or digital method.
